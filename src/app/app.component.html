States

Uttar Pradesh

Uttar Pradesh is known for its diverse and rich culinary heritage, featuring both vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes. Popular dishes include Awadhi Biryani, Lucknowi Biryani, Nihari, Tunday Kebab, and the street food like Chaat.

Karnataka

Karnataka is known for its diverse and flavorful cuisine. Some of the most famous dishes include Mysore Pak (a sweet made of besan, sugar, and ghee), Bisi Bele Bath (a lentil and rice dish), Ragi Mudde (finger millet balls), dosa and Neer Dosa.

Haryana

Haryana is known for its hearty and flavorful cuisine, featuring dishes like Singri Ki Sabzi, sarso ka saag Bajra Aloo Roti, Hara Dhania Cholia. Other popular items include Kachri Ki Sabzi, Mixed Dal, and the sweet dishes like Malpua and Mithe Chawal.

Bihar

Bihar is renowned for its diverse and flavorful cuisine, with Litti Chokha savory snacks being a quintessential dish. Other popular foods include Chana Ghugni, Dal Pitha, Kadhi Bari, and various sweets like Thekua, Malpua, and Khaja.
